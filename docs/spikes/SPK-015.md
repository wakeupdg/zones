# SPK-015: Same-axis chain merge (challenge C3)

Ticket: T18. Test: `EditorCommandTests.spk015Formula` (test-local helper, nothing in `Sources/`).

## Verdict
- Pass: the re-chaining formula is deterministic.
- Recorded, not applied. Merge stays sibling-only and AC-129 holds.
- Adopting it needs a follow-up ADR amending ADR-0003. That ADR is not written here.

## Formula
- Shape: `.split(axis, r1, .leaf, .split(axis, r2, .leaf, C))`. A and B are leaves, both splits share `axis`.
- Merging A and B gives `.split(axis, r1 + (1 - r1) * r2, .leaf, C)`.
- C moves up one level. Its own subtree and ratios are unchanged.
- Why: A takes `r1` of the parent, B takes `(1 - r1) * r2`. The merged Zone is their sum.
  C keeps `(1 - r1) * (1 - r2) = 1 - merged` of the parent, so its absolute extent does not change.
- Check: the test merges every pair in every Template at 1920x1080. The merged Zone frame equals
  the union of A and B, and every other Zone frame is unchanged.

## Scope
- Supported: the right-nested chain `A | (B | C)` with A and B leaves, found at any depth.
- Unsupported, no formula invented:
  - Grid 4 and Grid 9 vertical axis: the first child is a row (a split), not a leaf.
  - Grid 4 rows are `A | B` (no third part).
  - Columns 2 and Rows 2: `A | B`, already sibling merge (AC-129).
  - Focus 1: one leaf.
  - Any left-nested chain `(A | B) | C`: Templates never generate one.

## Undo
- Exact. Undo restores the stored pre-merge `EditorDraft` (a value copy), not an inverse computation.
