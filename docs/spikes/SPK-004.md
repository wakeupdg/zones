## Method

- 50 NSWindows tiled 10 by 5 (no overlap), every third borderless, sizes 55 to 95 percent of the cell. Titles are the probe's own ("probe-N"); no other app's names are read or requested.
- Check 1: `Int(window.windowNumber)` is a `kCGWindowNumber` in `CGWindowListCopyWindowInfo(.optionAll)` owned by the probe pid.
- Check 2: `CGWindowListCopyWindowInfo(.optionIncludingWindow, id)` with no companion option returns exactly one entry with that number and pid.
- Check 3: two listen-only taps (plain session and annotated session) on leftMouseDown. A synthetic mouseMoved plus leftMouseDown/Up is posted with `CGEvent.post(tap: .cghidEventTap)` at the window center (CG bounds). The field is read in the callback.
- Needed grant: the host process had the permission to create the taps; no Screen Recording used.

## Caveats (do not skip)

- The click events were SYNTHETIC (CGEventPost), not hardware. The annotated-versus-plain result is therefore proven for posted events only. A real trackpad event may be filled at the plain tap; the human check for AC-044 should read the field at both tap points once with a real click.
- One display, one Space, windows of one pid. Windows of other apps, other Spaces and mixed-DPI were not exercised.
- macOS 14 and 15 rows are not run.

## One-line consequence

On macOS 27.0.1 `windowNumber` is the CGWindowID and `optionIncludingWindow` alone is safe for `windows(ids:)`; `candidates(under:hint:)` may take the hint from the CGEvent field, but the production tap must be created at `.cgAnnotatedSessionEventTap` (the plain session tap saw 0 of 50), and the hit test must stay as the fallback.
