# SPK-014 Key status from a Carbon hotkey

**Verdict: recorded for macOS 27.0.1 only; macOS 14 and 15 are untested (no machine on those releases). The AC-023 expectation for 14 and 15 stays open until the table rows below are filled.**

- Fixes: AC-023 (T32). "Ordering front is required regardless" holds on the one OS tested.
- Host: MacBook Pro (Mac16,1, Apple M4), macOS 27.0.1 (26A434). Probe app is an accessory-policy process (mimics LSUIElement, ADR 0019), unsigned `swift build` binary.
- Tool: `.build/debug/HardwareProbe hotkey [--activate|--no-activate] --simulate --exit-after 9`.
- Setup deviation: the frontmost app was Calculator, not TextEdit. Chord and the following plain `z` key were posted with `CGEvent` (`--simulate`, Accessibility trusted), not typed on a physical keyboard. Re-run by hand with TextEdit frontmost on each OS before treating these as final.
- Handler: Carbon `RegisterEventHotKey`, Control+Option+Comma, `RegisterEventHotKey` returned `registered` (no TCC prompt). The handler calls `orderFrontRegardless()` on a titled window, plus `NSApp.activate()` in `--activate` mode.

## Raw log excerpts (macOS 27.0.1)

With activate: immediately after `orderFront`: `isVisible=true isKeyWindow=false NSApp.isActive=false frontmost=Calculator`; at +150 ms and +600 ms: `isKeyWindow=true isMainWindow=true NSApp.isActive=true frontmost=HardwareProbe`; typed `z`: `keyDown reached window: chars="z"`.

Without activate: at 0, +150 and +600 ms: `isVisible=true isKeyWindow=false NSApp.isActive=false onActiveSpace=true frontmost=Calculator`; no keyDown reached the window.

## What this means for AC-023 and T32

- On the OS tested, the window orders front on the current Space in both modes; it becomes key only after `NSApp.activate()`, and key status arrives asynchronously (check it after one run-loop turn, not synchronously after `activate`).
- Implication to confirm on 14 and 15: the hotkey handler must call `NSApp.activate()` before ordering the Settings window front if typing is to land; without it the window is front but not key.
- Do not hard-code "key on 14/15" in AC-023 until the four untested rows are filled.
