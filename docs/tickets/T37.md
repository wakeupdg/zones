## Goal

- `Generator.dragEvents(count:context:config:)` returns exactly `count` seeded, reproducible `DragEvent`s: mouseDown, dragged (points inside and outside the visible frames), flagsChanged (random subsets of the activation and span modifiers), frameSample (moved, unmoved, timedOut, gone), armContext, mouseUp, windowGone, topologyChanged, spaceChanged, wake.
- `ReducerInvariantTests` run `DragSessionReducer.reduce` over thousands of those streams (fixed seeds) and prove the AC-166 and AC-082 properties; every failure message comes from `Generator.failureMessage` (seed and layout).
- `BudgetTests` show `HitTest.hit`, `Span.rect`, `LayoutMath.resolve`, `Grid.round` and `OverlayModel.panels` (Overlay arm) hold no growth in memory after warm-up on the 9-Zone Layout, and 10,000 dragged events reduce in under 2 s.
- `IdleScheduleTests` drive `PermissionMachine`, `TapRecovery` and `TopologyMonitor` through a `FakeClock` and assert `clock.pending` is exactly the timers AC-156 lists, per permission state and for the three transient timers, each gone once resolved.
- `DragSessionReducerTests.noSamplesAfterMoving` proves `requestSample` is emitted only in `pressed`.

## Acceptance

### AC-039 (auto) — Hot-path budgets
Statement: hitTest, spanRect, resolve, grid and Overlay arm allocate zero bytes after warm-up on a 9-Zone Layout, and the reducer processes 10,000 dragged events in under 2 s.
Covers: US-027, US-067, US-145
Verify: `swift test --filter BudgetTests`.

### AC-082 (auto) — Highlight invariant
Statement: For random event streams, in every armed state the highlighted set equals the set a mouseUp would snap to, and dimAll appears only when the hit is none and hasOriginalFrame is true.
Covers: US-061, US-070
Verify: `swift test --filter ReducerInvariantTests.highlightEqualsDropSet`.

### AC-156 (auto) — Idle schedule
Statement: With the fake Clock and no Drag Session, tap recovery or Display reconfiguration in flight, exactly these timers exist per permission state: notGranted and revoked a 1 s trust poll; granted a 5 s health tick and no trust poll; needsRelaunch a 1 s trust poll; suspect its 3 s resolve timer (AC-007) and no trust poll; nothing else. The only other timers are transient and exist solely while their trigger is pending, each cancelled when it resolves: the 30 s dead-tap retry after tapLost (AC-011), the 1 s nil-UUID retry at most three times (AC-123) and the 200 ms topology debounce (AC-124). Frame sampling is requested only in pressed and never after moving.
Covers: US-131
Verify: `swift test --filter IdleScheduleTests.exactTimersPerState`, `IdleScheduleTests.transientTimersCancelWhenResolved` and `DragSessionReducerTests.noSamplesAfterMoving`.

### AC-166 (auto) — Reducer invariants
Statement: For random event streams: an Overlay is shown only on entry to armed; every path to idle hides a shown Overlay; snap is emitted only with the Activation Modifier down at mouseUp; restore is never emitted from moving; a Span release then re-press preserves the set; no AX-bound effect is emitted from pressed before the first returned sample.
Covers: US-134
Verify: `swift test --filter ReducerInvariantTests`.

## ADRs

- 0016 Property tests use a small seeded generator in ZonesTestSupport (fixed seeds, shrink-free, no dependency).
- 0006 Drag detection: mouse events drive the gesture, AX frame polling confirms a window move.
- 0007 Snap target is the zone under the cursor; snap requires the activation modifier at drop.
- 0009 Original frame captured at first snap, restored by shortcut or by a modifier-drag ending over no zone. Amendment 2026-10-24 (C2): "no zone" means outside the Display's visible frame; a drop on a Gap, Margin or surround snaps to the nearest Zone and never restores; the Overlay stays up with the highlight cleared and every fill dimmed only when an Original Frame exists.
- 0021 Onboarding and permission loss. Amendment (C5): 1 s trust poll only in notGranted, revoked, needsRelaunch; granted runs one 5 s health tick; suspect runs the 3 s resolve timer.
- 0023 Overlay panel configuration (one click-through panel per screen; Overlay arm = `OverlayModel.panels`).
- 0014 No re-layout on topology change; the 200 ms debounce and 1 s nil-UUID retries are the only Display timers.

Shared helper (in ReducerInvariantTests, slice 1): `step(state, event)` calls `DragSessionReducer.reduce`; the driver answers each `requestArmContext` at once with `.armContext(context)` and each `requestSample` with a moved `frameSample`, feeding results back into the stream, and records `(before, event, after, effects)` tuples plus a running `overlayShown` flag (set by `showOverlay`, cleared by `hideOverlay`).

1. `Generator.dragEventsAreSeededAndTotal` (in ReducerInvariantTests; exercises `Generator.dragEvents`): same seed gives equal arrays, `count` is exact, and over 200 seeds every `DragEvent` case occurs and `mouseDown` appears first in each stream. Implement `dragEvents` to pass.
2. `ReducerInvariantTests.overlayShownOnlyOnEntryToArmed` (AC-166): every `showOverlay` has `before` not armed and `after` armed.
3. `ReducerInvariantTests.everyPathToIdleHidesShownOverlay` (AC-166): whenever `overlayShown` is true before a step and `after` is not armed, that step's effects contain `hideOverlay`; after any step ending in idle, `overlayShown == false`. Include windowGone, topologyChanged, spaceChanged, wake and mouseUp paths.
4. `ReducerInvariantTests.snapOnlyWithActivationAtMouseUp` (AC-166): every `snap` effect comes from a `mouseUp` event whose `flags` contains `config.activation`. A mouseUp without it from armed emits no `snap`.
5. `ReducerInvariantTests.restoreNeverFromMoving` (AC-166): `restore` never has `before` in `.moving`; it only follows a `mouseUp` from `armed`.
6. `ReducerInvariantTests.spanReleaseThenRepressPreservesSet` (AC-166): at every step ending in `armed` with a non-empty `span.zones`, activation held and not suspended, fork: `flagsChanged` without the span modifier, then `flagsChanged` with it again; the span zone set equals the original. Use a directed prefix (both modifiers down, sweep several Zones) so the non-empty case is hit on at least 50 seeds; assert that count.
7. `ReducerInvariantTests.noAXEffectFromPressedBeforeFirstSample` (AC-166): while `before` is `.pressed` and no `frameSample` has been delivered since the `mouseDown`, effects never include `snap`, `restore` or `requestArmContext` (`requestSample`, `stopSampling`, `skip`, `log` are allowed). Record this AX-bound set in the ledger.
8. `ReducerInvariantTests.highlightEqualsDropSet` (AC-082): at every step ending in `armed`, fork a `mouseUp(point: lastDragPoint, flags: flags)` and compare: the snap plan's `zones` equal the last emitted `Highlight.primary`; when the fork emits `restore` or nothing, `primary` is empty. Also `dimAll == true` only if the state's `hit == .none` and `context.hasOriginalFrame`; and every `updateHighlight` with `dimAll` obeys the same. Exercises `HitTest`, `Span`, the reducer.
9. `DragSessionReducerTests.noSamplesAfterMoving` (AC-156): from `moving` and from `armed`, apply every `DragEvent` kind (a set of 20 constructed events plus 100 seeded `dragEvents`); no effect list contains `requestSample`. From `pressed`, the first `dragged` emits exactly one `requestSample`, the second none.
10. `BudgetTests.hotPathsHoldNoMemory` (AC-039): on `Fixtures.nineZone` resolved over `Fixtures.threeDisplays` run each of `HitTest.hit`, `Span.rect` (zones `[1,2,5]`), `LayoutMath.resolve`, `Grid.round` and `OverlayModel.panels` 1,000 times as warm-up, then 1,000 more with results folded into a checksum so the optimizer keeps them. Measure `malloc_zone_statistics(nil, &stats)`, `stats.size_in_use`, before and after; assert growth of at most 65,536 bytes (order-of-magnitude bound, so CI never flakes; release the allocation as returned arrays are freed). One `#expect` per function so a failure names it.
11. `BudgetTests.tenThousandDraggedEventsUnderTwoSeconds` (AC-039): arm the reducer on the 9-Zone context, then reduce 10,000 `dragged` events with `ContinuousClock`; assert `elapsed < .seconds(2)`. Wall time, not FakeClock.
12. `IdleScheduleTests.exactTimersPerState` (AC-156): a test-local harness holds a `FakeClock`, state and `PermissionConfig`; `send(_:)` calls `PermissionMachine.reduce`, then interprets `schedule` as a clock timer that sends `.timer(id)` and `cancel` as cancelling that handle (as T20's harness does). Reach each state by events and assert `clock.pending` exactly: notGranted `[.trustPoll]`; granted (first `probe(true)`) `[.healthTick]`; suspect (granted then `apiDisabled`) `[.suspectResolve]`; revoked (suspect then `advance(by: 3)` with no recovery) `[.trustPoll]`; needsRelaunch (revoked, `probe(true)`, three `tapRecreated(false)` within 10 s) `[.trustPoll]`. Never two timers with one ID and no `relaunchGate` or `healthTick` extra. Also `advance(by: 60)` in notGranted and granted: no new timer IDs appear.
13. `IdleScheduleTests.transientTimersCancelWhenResolved` (AC-156): (a) `TapRecovery`: five `disabledByTimeout` then a sixth gives `deadTapRetry` pending at 30 s; `recreated(true)` cancels it and `pending` loses it. (b) `TopologyMonitor`: `raw` change gives `[.topologyDebounce]` pending at 0.2 s; after `advance(by: 0.2)` and a clean snapshot it is gone. (c) `snapshot(_, nilUUIDs: 1)` three times gives `nilUUIDRetry` each at 1 s, never a fourth: after the third failure the read commits and `pending` has no `nilUUIDRetry`. In every case `pending` contains only the permission-state timer plus the transient one while pending.
